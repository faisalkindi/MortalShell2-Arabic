# Mortal Shell II — Terminology Proposal (lore-driven)

Every entry below is argued from **how the game uses the thing**, with the in-game English
quoted as evidence — not from a dictionary match. Human owner decides; nothing is frozen
until approved.

Inherited column = what the Asmar draft used (and what the current build ships).

---

## 1. Gloom — the core currency

> *"Inside the Mether's bones, lies her most potent gift, the **Blackmarrow**. And yet when
> released from her heavenly form, it's crudely called Gloom."*
> *"Consume Gloom to Strengthen The Harbinger."* · *"A wisp of Mether's breath which escaped the Gloom Siphon."*

**What it is:** a physical substance harvested from a dead goddess's marrow. You carry it,
spend it, drop it on death. "Gloom" is explicitly the *crude, common* name for a thing whose
true name is Blackmarrow.

| Candidate | Reading | Argument |
|---|---|---|
| **القَتام** ← recommended | qatām — dark, dusty gloom | A real Arabic word for gloom *as matter* (dust-dark), not absence of light. Earthy and crude — exactly the register of a commoner's nickname for a holy substance. Works as a countable resource. |
| الحَلَك | ḥalak — pitch blackness | Strong, but abstract; harder to read as a substance you hold. |
| السُّخام | sukhām — soot | Tangible and crude, but it says "soot", losing the marrow/holy origin. |
| ~~الظلمة~~ (inherited) | darkness | The dictionary sense of the English word. Says "absence of light" — the one thing Gloom is not. |

Related: **Blackmarrow → النُّخاع الأسود** (true name), **Gloomslayer → قاتل القَتام**,
**Gloom Siphon → مِصفاة القَتام**.

## 2. Shell — the possessable bodies

> *"the bond between Shell and bearer"* · *"We knew not what to call these fallen vessels"*
> *"Bring to Zhirelle, the Shell Keeper to sever your bond with a shell"*

**What it is:** the husk of a dead warrior that the Harbinger inhabits. A body, a frame, a vessel.

| Candidate | Reading | Argument |
|---|---|---|
| **الهَيكل** (pl. الهَياكل) ← recommended | haykal — frame, hull, skeletal structure | Reads as a body-frame left behind; carries weight and a faint sacred echo (هيكل = temple), which fits warriors venerated as relics. Shellkeeper → **حارس الهَياكل**. |
| الوِعاء | wiʿāʾ — vessel | Matches the game's own "fallen vessels" phrasing; slightly clinical. |
| الجُثمان | juthmān — corpse/remains | Accurate but grim-literal; loses the "you wear it" idea. |
| ~~الغِلاف~~ (inherited) | wrapper, envelope | Packaging vocabulary. A book has a غلاف. Nobody inhabits one. |

## 3. Tarstone / Tarforge / Tar

> *"Ounce of Tar."* · *"A slurry of spirits and Tar"* · *"Used to temper Tarstones at the Tarforge."*

**What it is:** Tar is a literal substance in this world (drunk, forged, hardened). Arabic
already has the exact word — **القار** — used for pitch/tar since classical texts.

| English | Recommended | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Tar | **القار** | The real word. No reason to invent one. |
| Tarstone | **حجر القار** | Says what it is; a player instantly understands. |
| Tarforge | **مِصهَر القار** | miṣhar = smelter/forge. |
| ~~inherited~~ | ~~تارستون / تارفورج~~ | Transliteration of a compound whose meaning is the whole point. Means nothing to an Arabic ear. |

## 4. Ova / Ovum — the sacred eggs

> *"we carved Her temples from these very walls to spread and protect Her Ova"* ·
> *"Cleanse Spoiled Ovum"* · *"I am the Ovum Keeper"*

| Candidate | Argument |
|---|---|
| **البَيضة / البُيوض** ← recommended | The sacred-egg meaning is load-bearing for the entire plot. Ovum Keeper → **حارس البُيوض**, Unblemished Ova → **البُيوض الطاهرة**, Spoiled Ovum → **بَيضة فاسدة**. |
| البَيضة المقدّسة | Adds "sacred" explicitly — heavier, but useful where reverence is the point. |
| ~~أوفا~~ (inherited) | Transliterated Latin. An Arabic player learns nothing; the religious weight evaporates. |

## 5. Seal — emblem and weapon

> *"Both symbol and weapon..."* · *"Equipping different Seals dramatically alters your Character's capabilities."*

| Candidate | Argument |
|---|---|
| **الخاتَم** ← recommended | khātam is the physical signet object (and carries sacred resonance, cf. خاتم النبيين). A thing you wear and wield. Slayer Seal → **خاتم القاتل**, Vatra's Seal → **خاتم فاترا**. |
| ~~الخَتم~~ (inherited ختم) | The *act* of stamping, or an office rubber-stamp. Wrong register for a ritual weapon. |

## 6. Harden — the signature mechanic

> *"Harden your flesh to prevent damage"* · *"Harros petrifies nearby enemies... While hardened..."*

| Candidate | Argument |
|---|---|
| **التحجُّر** ← recommended | Turning to stone. Matches what is shown on screen and the game's own "petrifies" language. Perfect Harden → **تحجُّر مثالي**. |
| التصلُّب | Generic stiffening; also the medical term for sclerosis. |

## 7. Glimpse — memory fragments

> *"Sift through their Glimpses… what they call 'memories.'"* · *"It was a Glimpse… more real than life itself."*

| Candidate | Argument |
|---|---|
| **الوَمضة** (pl. وَمَضات) ← recommended | A flash — a fragment of vision you can collect and spend. Tangible as an object, which the UI requires ("Spend a Glimpse"). |
| ~~لمحة~~ (inherited) | Dictionary match for "glance". Weak as a countable relic; reads as a passing look. |

## 8. Sester / Brether — the cult's invented kin-titles

> *"They arose as Sesters and brethers unbound from their flesh."* · *"Sester Genessa"* · *"High Sester"*

**What they are:** deliberately warped forms of *sister* and *brother* — the English is
misspelled on purpose. Transliterating them (سستر/بريذر) hands an Arabic reader a foreign
noise and loses the fact that these are kinship words, bent.

| Candidate | Argument |
|---|---|
| **أُخَيّة / أُخَيّ** ← recommended | Archaic diminutives of "sister/brother" — odd, old, faintly ritual to an Arabic ear, exactly like Sester/Brether is to an English one. High Sester → **الأُخَيّة الكُبرى**, Sester Genessa → **الأُخَيّة جينيسا**. |
| سِستر / بريذر (inherited) | Preserves foreignness, loses the kinship meaning entirely. Defensible only if we want the cult to sound alien rather than archaic. |

## 9. Confirmed keepers (inherited choices that survive scrutiny)

| Term | Arabic | Why it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Harbinger | **النذير** | Herald/omen-bearer. Exactly right, ominous register. |
| Beacon | **المنارة** | Lighthouse/minaret — a lit waypoint. The game even calls them "fallen vessels... surely Beacons"; منارة carries both signal and shrine. |
| Resolve | **العزيمة** | Willpower that fuels action. Correct and idiomatic. |
| Break (damage) | **الكَسر** | Posture-break. Reads correctly in combat text. |
| Marrow Keep | **حصن النخاع** | Consistent with Blackmarrow → النخاع الأسود. |
| Mether / Undermether | **ميثر / أندرميثر** | Proper divine name — transliteration is right for names. |
| Fallgrim, Egon, Gorf, Genessa, ... | transliterated | Personal and place names transliterate. Unchanged. |

## 10. The rule this establishes

- **Personal names and divine names** → transliterate (Egon, Genessa, Mether, Fallgrim).
- **Invented world-nouns whose meaning carries lore** → render into meaningful Arabic
  (Tarstone, Gloom, Ova, Shell, Glimpse, Seal).
- **Warped English words** → warp the Arabic equivalent, don't transliterate (Sester, Brether).

The test for every term: *does an Arabic player learn what this thing is from the word itself,
the way an English player does?*
