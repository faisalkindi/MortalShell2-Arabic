# Corpus-Only Speaker Notes (no web coverage)

Source: `01_extracted_strings.jsonl`, filtered by `speaker` field. All findings below are sourced strictly from in-game English lines — no web guides exist for these characters. Quotes are verbatim `source_en`.

## Egon (309 lines, `ST_Dialogue_Egon` + Lazlo scenes)
**Gender: male.** Self-identifies: "I am the Grand-Prince, Egon." Addressed as "Cousin…" and refers to himself as heir "to the Seat of Mammon" / "High Lord of Mammon."
**Voice:** Haughty, extortionate petty noble demanding "tribute" from the player ("Let's see your so-called 'tribute.'" / "A good servant should be seen… not heard."). Classist and casually cruel ("What are a few peasant lives compared to a single noble?" / "I'd butcher them all."). Register is mock-courtly, dripping with condescension ("Welcome back, my… 'friend'.").
**Relationship:** Runs a tribute/blackmail racket tied to Lazlo (many lines share `_Lazlo_` scene tags, e.g. accused of causing the "Peasants Revolt," judging a "thief" in front of the player). **Important cross-reference:** `BlisterBoi` independently self-identifies with the identical line "For I am the Grand-Prince Egon afterall" and "Remember. My father is the High King of Dannor" — BlisterBoi appears to be Egon at an earlier life stage (a memory/flashback), sharing scene-naming with Egon's Lazlo material (`BlisterBoi_Lazlo_Interact_01`, `BlisterBoi_MasterTree_*` matching the game's memory-tree pattern used elsewhere, e.g. `TielMemory`, `SarielMemory`). Treat Egon and BlisterBoi as the same named character (Egon, Grand-Prince/heir of Dannor) at two different points in the story — recommend a single glossary entry with a note on dual context.

## Gorf (294 lines, `ST_GorfDialogues` + `Dialogue_Dlg_Gorf_*`)
**Gender: male.** Confirmed indirectly: Hilga calls a baby frog "his father"'s son ("Listen to that ribbit! Takes after his father"), and Gorf's own lines discuss wanting "a mate" and losing his singing voice.
**Voice:** Comic, lovelorn frog-man NPC. Self-pitying and whimsical: "I think I might give up on singing and searching for love." / "It seems my song has attracted the wrong sort of company." Speech is full of frog puns ("Just got a 'frog' in my throat... so to speak.").
**Relationship:** Tied romantically/companionably to Hilga (referenced by her: "Hilga will set it up when she returns" / "she has some sort of surprise for me"). Also connected to a "Great Frógga" cult figure and fathers a baby frog late in his arc ("That's Daot!").

## Hilga (156 lines, `ST_Dialogue_Hilga`) / HilgaTransformed (17 lines)
**Gender: female.** Confirmed via Gorf's line about her ("she returns"). Pre-transformation Hilga speaks in broken, clipped syntax dropping articles: "What? You want drink? Then drink." / "All you do is take, take, take." Blunt, brothel/tavern-keeper register, insults the player crudely ("you greedy tar-son," "unwashed wench," "cod-pieced cukold").
**HilgaTransformed** is confirmed as the SAME character post-transformation — she references her past actions directly: "I only needed blood from the Great Frógga himself," "You know... I tried to 'change' you, too," and grieves a lost partner "Harwald" ("If only Harwald was here"). Speech becomes even more fragmented/inhuman ("No more human desires." / "One with nature." / "I am… complete."), consistent with a body-horror transformation via frog blood. She explicitly says she will "take care of Gorf" now, implying Gorf's fate is now in her hands.

## Blacksmith (366 lines, `ST_Dialogue_Blacksmith`) — CONFIRMED = Franz
Self-identifies directly: "Well, I'm Franz." Matches `ST_Core_NpcNames/Blacksmith = Franz` and credits role `ST_Role_Franz`. This is the SAME "Franz the smith" noted in `04_game_context.md` — not a separate character.
**Gender: male.** Addressed/self-refers with male-coded lines; has a son ("What's this? Who are you? What did you do to my son?").
**Voice:** Gruff, weary old craftsman, affectionate toward his forge ("Tarforge," personified as "she") which he treats almost like a companion/daughter figure ("She's good company at least. Don't hold grudges." / "Careful. She's hungerin' fiercely."). Calls the player "laddie." Melancholic undertone about a past "accident" that broke Tarforge and estranged his son.
**Relationship:** Central hub-NPC for weapon upgrades via Tarforge; personal subplot about reconciling with an estranged son runs through his dialogue.

## Vlas (127 lines) + VlasEvent (86 lines)
**Gender: likely male** (name convention, signs a letter "Sincerely, Vlas"), but no in-line pronoun from another speaker was found in the sampled lines to confirm grammatically — flag as unconfirmed if strict pronoun evidence is required.
**Voice:** Formal, mercantile, guarded — a trader/"commerce" NPC ("Care for a little commerce?" / "Have we engaged in commerce before?"). Under stress reveals guilt and moral conflict over past violence: "I refused their proposals, their bribes." / "But I'm no butcher. No murderer." / "I tried to avoid bloodshed."
**VlasEvent** is a distinct quest-chain (escape plot) sharing the same voice/register: he distills "Gloom" (a drug/resource, "Do you know how much Tar it takes to make an ounce of Gloom?"), plans to flee with his companion animal "Mutton" ("Mutton and I will depart in the night"), and admits to killing bandits in self-defense ("But I made his suffering end quickly"). His full arc is much larger than "minor" — he runs an escape/rescue questline tied to a captured companion (Mutton) and past cult/bandit entanglements. Recommend upgrading his glossary priority from "minor."

## BlisterBoi (124 lines)
**Gender: male** — see Egon note above; self-identifies as "Grand-Prince Egon," son of "the High King of Dannor."
**Voice:** Petulant, self-important child/youth noble playing at kingship — a comic, pompous register distinct from adult Egon's menace: "For I am the Grand-Prince Egon afterall." / "Choose wisely. You could do well in my court-to-be." / "Withdrawing your contribution? That is... unwise." Runs the same tribute-extortion "game" as adult Egon but in a mock/lesser form (a child playing prince), fitting a comic-relief framing despite thematically mirroring Egon's corruption.
**Relationship:** Same lineage/identity thread as Egon; also tied to Lazlo (`BlisterBoi_Lazlo_Interact_01`).

## Merchant (193 lines) — CONFIRMED = Merrick
Self-identifies: "Just come visit your new friend, Merrick." Confirmed further by unrelated flashback scenes (`ProximaMemory`) where other characters address him directly: "You are Merrick." / "What do you want, Merrick?" Matches `ST_Core_NpcNames/Merchant = Merrick`; credits list "Merrick, Basil" (voice actor Basil).
**Gender: male** (name + direct address).
**Voice:** Wry, working-class shopkeeper patter with dark comic self-awareness about resurrection mechanics: "Well, look at this, your Mether brought me back." / "I won't pretend I understand it all, buuuuut…". Uses casual contractions ("gotta," "buyin'," "brev").
**Relationship:** Grieving/attached to a deceased hunter named **Proxima**, whom the player can revive via a quest ("All you gotta do is bring Proxima back to life." / "I wonder… what kinda life she or maybe even we could've had."). This is a significant emotional subplot not previously documented.

## BanishedCultist (89 lines)
**Gender: undetermined from lines** — self-reference only as "I"/"me"; no gendered self-pronoun found. Refers to the Mether as "she" and the "Sacred/Foul Passenger" as "he," consistent with the rest of the corpus.
**Voice:** Fervent, quasi-religious, invites the player to physically harvest their heart as an offering ("Will you not reach into my heart… and feel it for yourself?"). Devotional and eerie register, addresses player as "Harbinger" and "friend."

## HubCultist (46 lines)
**Gender: undetermined from lines** — same caveat as above (only third-party pronouns present, e.g. "He killed my only friend").
**Voice:** Penitent, self-flagellating devotee grieving a friend killed by "Prophet Smert," seeking punishment/redemption from the player: "If I must die… then let it be by your hand." / "I have many failings… to atone for." Calls the player "Harbinger" and "sester" is used as an in-fiction gender-neutral(?) title for fellow devotees ("We know you will not fail, sester.") — note "sester"/"brether" appear to be the cult's own in-universe honorifics, not standard English gendered terms; do not map 1:1 to "sister/brother" without further corpus check.

## UnderMether (73 lines, `Dialogue_Dlg_UnderMether_Tower_Interact` etc.) and Undermether (28 lines, `Slice3Opens`/`Midpoint`/`PostFinalBoss`)
These two namespaces (case difference: `UnderMether` vs `Undermether`) both carry direct first-person lines from what reads as the same maternal, egg-tied entity — very likely the SAME character as the narrative "Undermether" deity described elsewhere as "she/her," simply split across two scene-authoring passes (an interactive "Tower" voice line-set and a separate story-beat/boss line-set), not two different entities. Evidence: both use identical maternal/kin language — "my children flourished," "each ovum you discover gives me hope," "you and I serve our kin-to-be," "we must send the Ova to the heavens." A 3-line `Undermehter` (typo'd namespace) duplicates two Undermether lines verbatim, confirming it's a stray/duplicate, not a new character.
**Voice:** Mournful, ancient, maternal-cosmic register — laments being "stranded" on this world, refers to a corrupting "wrongness" that "festered" in her own flesh, and to the player as someone who "can be strong, in ways that I'm not." Distinct from the cult's worship-object framing ("Blessed Mether") — this voice is intimate, regretful, first-person, addressing the Harbinger directly rather than being invoked in third person by worshippers.
**Relationship:** Deep tie to the "Ova"/eggs the player collects throughout the game and to "the Passenger" (called "Foul Passenger" here, an antagonist force inside her).

## Grisha (22 lines, `Dialogue_Dlg_Grisha_*`)
**Not a character speaking English dialogue — it is an animal companion.** All 22 lines are bracketed non-verbal sound cues: "(sniffs cautiously)," "(growls curiously)," "*lets out a welcoming bark*," "(whimpers)," "(burps)." This is a dog/hound companion NPC (a "Hunter's" pet, per scene names `ReactAfterCageOpens_HuntersFriendly`), not a spirit or the "Grisha Remnant" collectible resource from the web guide — those are unrelated homonyms. No gender is linguistically determinable (no pronouns at all; note that other speakers do call generic wild creatures in the setting "Grisha" as a species/monster name, e.g. ImmortalBrigand: "I feel strong as a Grisha anyway" — so "Grisha" is also a creature-type noun in this game's bestiary, separate from this named companion dog).

## Shellkeeper (63 lines) — CONFIRMED = Zhirelle
Self-identifies: "I am Zhirelle, the Shellkeeper." Matches `ST_Core_Menu_Shellkeeper/ShellkepperNPCName = Zhirelle` and credits. Confirms `04_game_context.md`'s identification.
**Gender:** Not resolved by a self-referential pronoun in the sampled lines (speaks only in "I"), but the name Zhirelle and established identity (per prior game-context research, "she/her") stands; no contradicting evidence found here.
**Voice:** Detached, philosophical, treats the player and dead NPCs' souls ("shells") as specimens for a cosmic "Great Question" she is investigating: "I am simply another curious traveller." / "I am a gatherer." / "This one had a big heart. And yet betrayal filled it with rage." She delivers eulogy-like character summaries when the player collects/inspects each dead NPC's "shell" (e.g. Sariel: "I would not pursue this… husk of a man. His very presence spreads rot and ruin."; Eredrim: "A cruel, loathsome knight. He sought redemption until the end.").
**Relationship:** Neutral-to-detached toward the Harbinger, allied only situationally ("for a moment… our goals have aligned"); references Genessa directly in several scenes (`InteractGenessa_01/02/03`).

## Cross-cutting corrections/flags for the glossary
- **Blacksmith = Franz** (confirmed, same person, not a second smith).
- **Merchant = Merrick** (confirmed; has a named grief subplot re: Proxima, upgrade from "shopkeeper flavor" to a character with an arc).
- **Egon and BlisterBoi are the same named character** ("Grand-Prince Egon," heir to Dannor) at two different points/contexts — merge or cross-link glossary entries rather than treating as unrelated.
- **Vlas/VlasEvent total ~213 lines** with a real questline (Gloom-brewing, an escape plot, guilt over violence) — should not be tagged "minor."
- **UnderMether / Undermether / Undermehter are one entity** split across inconsistent namespace casing — same feminine deity as the narrative "Undermether," not a separate tower-voice.
- **Grisha (dialogue speaker) is an animal companion (dog), not a person** and is unrelated to the "Grisha Remnant" collectible item found via web research — same word, two different referents (a named pet vs. a generic monster-species/resource noun used elsewhere in dialogue).
- Several minor speakers (ImmortalBrigand, BanishedCultist, HubCultist) have **no self-referential gendered pronoun** in their own lines — gender should be left unspecified/neutral in translation unless later corpus passes surface stronger evidence.
